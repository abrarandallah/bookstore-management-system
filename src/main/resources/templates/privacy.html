Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language description of what this specific app actually does with your data - not a law firm's template. It isn't legal advice, and whoever operates this instance should have it reviewed before relying on it for a real deployment with real users.
What we collect
  • Account info: username, email address, and a hashed (never plaintext) password
  • An avatar image, if you choose to upload one
  • Your reading activity: which books you've bookmarked, your reading progress and finished-book history, and any reviews or ratings you post
  • Basic technical data needed to keep the site working: your session cookie, and your IP address is used briefly to rate-limit repeated login attempts
What we don't do
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing your data with advertisers
  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts
  • No sharing your data with anyone outside what's needed to run the app itself (e.g. an email provider, solely to send you verification and password-reset emails)
Cookies

Just one: a session cookie that keeps you logged in. It's required for the site to function and isn't used for tracking or advertising.

Your data, your control

You can change your password or delete your account entirely, at any time, from Settings - deleting your account removes your reviews, reading history, and bookmarked books along with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to whoever's running this instance.
