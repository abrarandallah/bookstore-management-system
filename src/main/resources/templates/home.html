About BookStore

Most books have a handful of ideas worth remembering, wrapped in a few hundred pages of story, examples, and repetition. BookStore strips that down: every book here is distilled into 1–10 short takeaways — a few sentences each, capturing the core idea rather than the full argument.

It's meant for a first taste of a book you're deciding whether to read in full, or for when you just want the useful part without the time commitment. A typical book takes a few minutes to read here, not a few hours.

What you can do
  • Browse and search the shelf, sorted however's useful to you
  • Read any book's takeaways in the built-in reader, and pick up right where you left off
  • Bookmark books to your own "My Books" list, and track finished books in your reading history
  • Rate and review books, and earn achievements as you read
  • Librarians can register new books and their takeaways, including bulk import

Have feedback or found something broken? Reach out to whoever's running this instance — there's no dedicated support line here, it's a small independent project.
